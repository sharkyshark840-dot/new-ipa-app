# Safety and privacy scope

## Purpose

This prototype provides guided visual-awareness exercises for adults. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, screen for, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any eye condition.

## Required user-facing language

Every session entry and completion screen must state:

> This session is for general awareness only. It does not diagnose eye conditions or replace an eye examination. Stop if you feel discomfort, and contact a qualified eye-care professional if you have concerns.

## Camera and microphone boundaries

- The current prototype does not open the camera, capture eye images, record audio, or use speech recognition.
- Future camera or microphone access requires an explicit consent screen, a data-retention decision, on-device processing where feasible, security review, and clinical/regulatory review before any health claim.
- Do not infer a condition from camera, microphone, motion, answers, or session performance.

## Product boundaries

- Users must be 18 or older.
- Do not show a medical score, pass/fail result, or diagnosis-like label.
- Do not collect health information, location, identifiers, or contact details in this prototype.
